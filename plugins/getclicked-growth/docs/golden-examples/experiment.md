## The Bet

Every onboarding tool assigns tasks to new hires. Nobody coaches the manager. We're testing whether "manager coaching" positioning generates trial signups via Google Ads at a viable CPA.

---

## Hypothesis

**If** we run Google Ads on onboarding software and competitor-intercept keywords with manager-coaching messaging, **then** VP People and HR leaders will start free trials at CPA < $500, **because** every HR leader knows their managers skip onboarding check-ins and new hires suffer in silence.

---

## Success Criteria

| Condition | Metric | Threshold |
|:---------:|--------|-----------|
| **WIN** | Cost per trial signup | < $500 over 30 days / $5,000 spend (min 10 trials) |
| **KILL** | CPA | > $800 after $2,500 spend |
| **KILL** | CTR on any ad group | < 1.5% after 2,000 impressions |
| **PROTECT** | Landing page bounce rate | < 70% |

## Pre-Committed Decisions

- **If it wins:** Scale to $8K/mo. Build onboarding content hub (SEO). Create retargeting audience.
- **If one ad group wins:** Double down on the winner, kill the loser.
- **If it fails on CTR:** Positioning problem. Test "time-to-productivity" or "reduce 90-day attrition" angles.
- **If it fails on CPA (good CTR):** Landing page problem. A/B test structure and CTA.
