## Critical issues (fix immediately)

### 1. Missing meta descriptions on 60% of pages

**Impact:** Google auto-generates descriptions for 9 of 15 indexed pages. Auto-generated snippets average 1.8% CTR vs. 3.2% for custom descriptions. At ~2,400 impressions/mo, that's 34 lost clicks/month.

**Fix:** Write unique meta descriptions for all 15 pages. Lead with the benefit, include a verb, stay under 155 characters. **Effort:** Low -- 2-3 hours of copywriting.

### 2. Homepage mobile load time: 4.2 seconds

**Impact:** Fails Core Web Vitals (2.5s threshold). Mobile users (48% of traffic) are 2.5x more likely to bounce at >3s. Lighthouse mobile score: 58.

**Fix:** Compress hero image to WebP (<200KB), lazy-load below-fold images, defer non-critical JS. **Target:** LCP under 2.5s. **Effort:** Medium -- 3-4 hours.
