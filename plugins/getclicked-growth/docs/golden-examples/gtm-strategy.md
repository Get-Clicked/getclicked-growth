# Go-to-Market Strategy -- Bloom

> **Status: Draft** | Generated by /gtm on 2026-03-19

Bloom is at early GTM-Fit with 180 customers, $5K/month ad budget, and a content gap to fill. The strategic question: how does a 12-person company go from "product that works" to 500 customers by year-end? Three channels -- onboarding content hub (SEO), Google Ads on commercial keywords, and SHRM/HR community partnerships.

---

**Stage:** Early GTM-Fit | **Budget:** $5,000/mo (ads) + team time | **Team:** 1 marketer + founder

---

## The Three-Channel Strategy

### Channel 1: Onboarding Content Hub (SEO) -- The Growth Loop

**The play:** Become the definitive onboarding resource. "Employee onboarding checklist" gets 2,900/mo with competition index 15. Nobody owns it authoritatively.

**The loop:** HR manager searches "onboarding checklist" -> finds Bloom's guide -> downloads template -> enters email nurture -> starts trial -> converts. Content compounds. Every article is a permanent lead gen asset.

**Budget:** $1,000/mo (content production) + team time.

### Channel 2: Google Ads (SEM) -- The Immediate Pipeline

**The play:** Capture commercial-intent buyers searching "employee onboarding software" (6,600/mo, $12.40 CPC). Competitor intercept on "BambooHR alternative" (1,300/mo). The ceiling is ~$8K/mo before diminishing returns.

**Budget:** $5,000/mo. Scale to $8K only if CPA < $600.

### Channel 3: HR Community Partnerships -- The Trust Pipeline

**The play:** SHRM chapters, People Ops Slack groups, HR podcasts. VP People trusts peer recommendations over ads. Sponsor the People Ops community newsletter ($500/issue, 12K subscribers). Guest on 2-3 HR podcasts/month.

**Budget:** $1,000-2,000/mo.

### Month 3: Systematize

## The Big Bet

Bloom's distribution advantage: the **manager coaching angle.** Every competitor talks compliance and task completion. Nobody says "your managers are the onboarding experience." Own that narrative and you become the default choice.
