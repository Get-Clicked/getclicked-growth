# Brand Strategy

> **Status: Draft** | Generated by /brand on 2026-03-19

Bloom coaches managers, not just assigns tasks. Your HRIS tracks paperwork, Bloom tracks whether your new hire is actually ramping. BambooHR is a system of record. Enboarder is enterprise-only. Bloom is the mid-market onboarding tool that measures time-to-productivity.

---

## Positioning Statement

For VP of People at companies growing 20-50% annually, Bloom is the employee onboarding platform that cuts time-to-productivity from 90 days to 30 -- because automated workflows, manager coaching nudges, and milestone tracking replace the shared Google Doc nobody maintains.

---

## Competitive Positioning

- **We are:** The onboarding tool that coaches managers and measures outcomes.
- **We are NOT:** An HRIS. A compliance tool. A cheaper BambooHR.
- **Against:** BambooHR/Rippling (onboarding is a feature), Enboarder (enterprise-only), spreadsheets (the real competitor).

---

## Voice and Tone

| Attribute | What It Means | Example |
|-----------|--------------|---------|
| Specific | Numbers and outcomes, never vague claims | "30 days to productive, not 90" not "accelerate onboarding" |
| Manager-aware | Always acknowledge that managers make or break onboarding | "Coaches your managers through day 1, 7, 30, 60, 90" |
| Anti-HR-speak | Plain language, no jargon | "New hire feels lost" not "suboptimal employee experience" |

**Forbidden:** "Comprehensive," "leverage," "empower," "best-in-class," "AI-powered" (without specifics), "transform your onboarding."

---

## Messaging Pillars

| Pillar | Core Message | Use When |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Speed to productivity | "30 days to productive, not 90" | Homepage hero, ads, sales decks |
| Manager coaching | "Coaches your managers, not just your new hires" | Differentiation, competitor comparisons |
| Measurable outcomes | "See who's ramping and who's struggling -- before they quit" | Analytics pages, CFO-facing content |

---

## Boilerplate

**One-liner:** Bloom gets new hires productive in 30 days, not 90.

---

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already have BambooHR" | BambooHR tracks paperwork. Bloom tracks whether your new hire is actually productive. They're complementary -- Bloom integrates with BambooHR in one click. |
| "Managers won't use it" | That's the whole point. Bloom nudges managers at the right moments with specific actions. No training needed. If they can read a Slack message, they can use Bloom. |
| "What's the ROI?" | Companies using Bloom see 40% reduction in 90-day attrition. At $15K average cost-per-bad-hire, that's $150K saved per 10 hires that would have churned. |

---

## Guardrails

- Lead with outcome, never technology. Never position as replacing HRIS. If a VP People would roll her eyes, rewrite it.
