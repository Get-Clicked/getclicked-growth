# VP of People at a Scaling Startup

> **Status: Draft** | Generated by /context on 2026-03-19

---

## Demographics

- **Role:** VP of People / Head of HR
- **Company:** 200-2,000 employees, Series B-D, growing 20-50% annually
- **Age:** 34-48
- **Income:** $140K-$210K
- **Reports to:** CEO or COO
- **Team size:** 3-8 (People Ops, recruiting, HRBP)

---

## Situation

Hired to "build the people function" 12-18 months ago. Recruiting engine is running but retention is the new crisis -- 90-day attrition hit 18% last quarter. New hires report feeling "lost" for their first month. Managers complain they don't have time for proper onboarding. The CEO just asked why Q3 hires are underperforming.

---

## Pain Points

- Onboarding is a shared Google Doc with 47 tasks, half outdated. Nobody owns it.
- Managers skip check-ins because there's no system nudging them. New hires suffer in silence.
- IT provisioning takes 2-3 days. New hire's first day is watching compliance videos on a loaner laptop.
- No data on whether onboarding is actually happening -- just anecdotal "it went fine" from managers.
- Already paying for BambooHR but its onboarding module is a checkbox exercise, not an experience.

---

## How They Search

- Problem-first: "how to reduce new hire turnover," "onboarding best practices 2026"
- Tool search: "employee onboarding software," "BambooHR onboarding alternative"
- Template search: "onboarding checklist template," "new hire 90-day plan"
- Platforms: Google, LinkedIn posts from other HR leaders, SHRM forums, People Ops Slack communities

---

## Objections

- "We already have BambooHR" -- integration reliability matters, not replacement
- "Managers won't use another tool" -- if it adds work, it's dead on arrival
- "What's the ROI?" -- needs hard numbers for CFO: retention improvement, time saved per hire
- "We're about to switch HRIS" -- timing objection, wants to wait

---

## What Resonates

- "30 days to productivity, not 90" -- concrete, measurable, tied to business outcome
- "Coaches your managers, not just your new hires" -- this is the wedge. Nobody else says it.
- Customer stories from similar-sized companies -- social proof at her scale
- Data dashboards she can show the CEO -- "here's our onboarding completion rate by team"

---

## What Falls Flat

- "Comprehensive onboarding platform" -- means nothing specific
- Feature lists without outcomes -- she doesn't care about "200+ integrations"
- "AI-powered onboarding" -- she's heard this from every vendor this quarter
- ROI calculators with fantasy assumptions -- she'll run her own numbers
