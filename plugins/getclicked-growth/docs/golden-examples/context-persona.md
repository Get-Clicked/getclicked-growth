# District SPED Director

## Demographics
- Age 40-55, typically female
- $75-110K salary
- Master's or Ed.D. in Special Education
- Oversees 504/IEP compliance across 5-50+ buildings
- Reports to superintendent or assistant superintendent

## Situation
- OCR complaints are increasing. Board is asking about compliance posture.
- Post-COVID 504 caseloads have exploded — more anxiety, ADHD, behavioral plans than ever
- Already owns a compliance tool (Frontline, Embrace, Go504) for plan writing
- The gap: plans exist on paper but daily execution is invisible. No way to know if accommodations are actually being provided.

## Pain Points
- Can't answer "is this 504 plan being followed?" without calling the building
- Incident reconstruction takes hours — piecing together emails, texts, nurse logs
- Parents escalate to the district when they feel uninformed. These calls eat the director's day.
- Substitutes and new staff have no context on students with health/behavior plans

## How They Search
- "504 compliance software" / "504 plan management"
- "IEP management software" / "SPED software"
- Frontline, Embrace, SpedTrack by name (already shopping)
- Conference sessions and peer recommendations (NASET, CEC)
- Google searches after an OCR complaint or parent escalation

## Objections
- "We already have Frontline/Embrace for 504s — why do we need another tool?"
- "Budget is tight — I need to justify this to the board"
- "Will teachers actually use it? We can barely get them to fill out existing forms"
- "Does it integrate with our SIS?"

## What Resonates
- "This doesn't replace your compliance tool — it fills the gap between the plan and the daily reality"
- Audit-ready documentation that holds up to OCR review
- Reduction in parent escalation calls to the district office
- Real data on whether accommodations are actually happening

## What Falls Flat
- "Better communication" — too vague, sounds like another app they won't use
- Feature lists — they want outcomes, not features
- Anything that implies their current compliance tool is inadequate (they chose it)
