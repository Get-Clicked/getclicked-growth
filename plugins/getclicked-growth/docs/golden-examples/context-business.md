## Product
FERPA-compliant communication platform for school health teams and families of students with chronic and behavioral health conditions.

Three core features:
- **Student Logbook** — shared timeline of care notes from nurses, counselors, and teachers. Time-stamped, centralized, audit-ready.
- **Alerts & Follow-Ups** — smart reminders tied to 504 plans, behavior plans, and incident tracking. Auto-routes to the right staff and parents.
- **Secure Parent Messaging** — compliant communication channel. No app download required for families.

## Value Proposition
Every 504 plan and IEP has a gap between the plan on paper and the plan in action. Invincible closes that gap. Staff log care notes in seconds, alerts route automatically, and the whole care team — including parents — sees the same timeline. When someone asks "what happened?" the answer is documented, timestamped, and audit-ready.

**Positioning vs. market:**
- Compliance tools (Frontline, Go504, SpedTrack, Embrace) manage the plan *document* — forms, workflows, deadlines, OCR audits
- Health record tools (Magnus Health, SNAP Health Center) manage *records* — immunizations, physicals, paper-to-digital forms
- Invincible manages the plan in *action* — daily coordination between nurse, teacher, counselor, and parent

## Founding Story
Founded by Bob Weishar, diagnosed with type 1 diabetes at 18. Spent a year interviewing 100+ families and school staff across multiple states. Consistent finding: families struggle to build and coordinate effective care teams across home and school. Communication is fragmented across texts, emails, paper logs, and people's heads. Nobody has the full picture.
