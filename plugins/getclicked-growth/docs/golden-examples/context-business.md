> **Status: Draft** | Generated by /context on 2026-03-19

Bloom is employee onboarding software for mid-market companies (200-2,000 employees). The pitch: new hires reach full productivity in 30 days instead of 90. HR teams save 12 hours per new hire on manual onboarding tasks. $8/employee/month, no setup fee, 14-day free trial.

---

## Business

Bloom (trybloom.io) automates the first 90 days of employment -- task assignments, manager check-ins, IT provisioning, compliance training, and culture integration. Founded 2024. 180 customers, mostly Series A-C tech companies.

---

## Products and Services

- **Onboarding Workflows** -- automated task sequences triggered by HRIS start date. Assigns IT setup, manager intros, training modules, and compliance forms.
- **Manager Coaching** -- nudges managers at day 1, 7, 30, 60, 90 with specific actions. "Schedule your 1:1 with Sarah" not "check in with your new hire."
- **New Hire Portal** -- single place for everything: org chart, team wiki, benefits enrollment, IT requests. No more "where do I find X?" Slack messages.
- **Analytics** -- time-to-productivity scores, completion rates, manager engagement, early attrition correlation.

---

## Audience

**Primary:** VP of People / Head of HR at mid-market companies scaling headcount 20-50% annually.

**Key Segments:**
- HR leaders at 200-2,000 employee companies with no dedicated onboarding tool
- People Ops teams using spreadsheets and Notion docs to manage onboarding
- Companies with high 90-day attrition (>15%) looking for root cause

**Pain Points:** New hires feel lost for weeks. Managers forget check-ins. IT provisioning takes 3 days. Compliance training falls through cracks. No visibility into whether onboarding is actually happening.

**How They Search:** "employee onboarding software," "onboarding automation," "new hire checklist tool," "reduce new hire turnover," "time to productivity."

---

## Value Proposition

New hires productive in 30 days, not 90. Bloom automates task assignments, manager nudges, and compliance tracking so HR teams stop chasing spreadsheets and start measuring what matters.
