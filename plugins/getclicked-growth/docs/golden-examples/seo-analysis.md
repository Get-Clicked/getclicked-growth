## Strategic Insights

1. **Manager coaching content is completely uncontested.** "How to onboard a new employee" (1,300/mo, comp index 8) has no product page ranking. Bloom should own this the way HubSpot owns "inbound marketing."

2. **Integration pages are the biggest growth lever.** BambooHR's 12K ranked keywords come primarily from integration pages. Bloom has 40+ integrations with zero SEO pages.

3. **Blog drives traffic but not trials.** 2,100 ranked keywords via blog, mostly informational. Posts need stronger CTAs and trial conversion pathways.
