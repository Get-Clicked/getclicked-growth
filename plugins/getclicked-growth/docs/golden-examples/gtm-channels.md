> **Status: Draft** | Generated by /gtm on 2026-03-19

Three inner-ring channels: onboarding content hub (SEO), Google Ads on commercial keywords, and HR community partnerships. Everything else waits. HRIS partnerships scored highest (27/35) but need 500+ customers before BambooHR takes the request seriously.

## Inner Ring -- Test Immediately

### 1. Content Marketing / SEO (Onboarding Content Hub)

**Why:** "New hire onboarding checklist" gets 2,900/mo with competition index 15. "Employee onboarding software" gets 6,600/mo. Nobody owns authoritative onboarding content. The content-to-trial path is strong: HR manager finds guide -> downloads template -> enters nurture -> starts trial.

- **Estimated CAC:** $80-200 (content amortized over organic lifetime)
- **Volume:** 15,000+/mo addressable organic traffic
- **Time to signal:** 3-6 months for rankings. Template downloads start immediately.

|---------|--------|-----|-----------|
| employee onboarding software | 6,600 | $12.40 | 38 |
| new hire onboarding checklist | 2,900 | $4.20 | 15 |
| how to onboard a new employee | 1,300 | $5.60 | 8 |

### 2. Google Ads (SEM) -- Immediate Pipeline

**Why:** Commercial-intent buyers searching "onboarding software" are actively shopping. Competitor intercept captures BambooHR evaluators. Not a new test -- an optimization play on known demand.

- **Estimated CAC:** $400-600 at 5-6% CVR
- **Volume:** ~8,000/mo relevant commercial keywords
- **Time to signal:** Immediate
- **Scalability:** Ceiling at ~$8K/mo

### 3. HR Community Partnerships -- Trust Pipeline

**Why:** VP People trusts peer recommendations. People Ops Slack (8K members), SHRM chapters, HR podcasts. Sponsor newsletters, guest on podcasts, contribute to communities.

- **Estimated CAC:** $200-500 (sponsorship cost per attributed trial)
- **Volume:** 5-15 qualified trials/month
- **Time to signal:** 1-2 months
