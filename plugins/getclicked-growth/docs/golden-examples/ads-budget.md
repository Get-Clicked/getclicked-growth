## CPC Inflation Buffer

DataForSEO CPC data reflects historical averages. Google Ads CPCs inflate ~12-13%/year. Actual launch CPCs may be 10-15% higher than the numbers above. Budget allocation accounts for this — we bid to the high end of DataForSEO ranges.

## Budget Reallocation Rules

Per EXP-001 timeline:
- **Week 1 (MAR 3-10):** Run all 3 groups. Gather baseline CTR and impression data.
- **Week 2 (MAR 10-17):** Kill any ad group with CTR < 1.5% after 2,000 impressions. Reallocate budget to surviving groups.
- **Week 3-4 (MAR 17-APR 2):** If CPA > $400 after $1,500 spend, pause and reassess. Otherwise, let it run to $3K decision point.
