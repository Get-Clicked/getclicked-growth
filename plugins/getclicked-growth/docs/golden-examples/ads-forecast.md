## The Honest Take

First-month ROAS is negative at every budget level. Three reasons it's still worth it:

1. **LTV makes the math work.** Average customer stays 24 months. A $476 CAC against $3,840 LTV is 8:1 payback.
2. **You're buying data.** First $5K teaches you which keywords and ad groups convert. That makes everything cheaper.
3. **"Manager onboarding" keywords are uncontested.** $4-6 CPCs vs. $12+ for generic "onboarding software." Bloom's differentiator is also its cheapest acquisition channel.

---

## Recommendation

Start at **$5,000/month**. After 6 weeks, reallocate to winning ad groups and consider $8K if CPA is trending below $600.
