Revenue depends entirely on demo-to-close rate, which is unknown. For reference:

## What Moves the Numbers

1. **Negative keyword quality** is the biggest lever for this campaign. Competitor intercept terms carry heavy navigational noise. Better negatives = fewer wasted clicks = lower CPA.
2. **Landing page match** — persona-matched pages with clear Calendly CTA will determine CVR.
3. **Ad group pruning** — if Private School Health performs, it could absorb more budget at $0.66-$1.50 CPC. If District Competitor Intercept underperforms (navigational noise too high), pause and reallocate.
4. **Time of year matters** — 504/IEP searches spike Feb-March (IEP season) and Aug-Oct (back to school). We're launching at a seasonal peak.

## Assumptions

- CVR benchmarks: EdTech/SaaS typically 3-5% on demo landing pages. Conservative = 2-3%, optimized = 3-5%.
- CPC data: DataForSEO (2026-03-03). Actual CPCs will vary with Quality Score and auction competition.
- Quality adjustment: 30% of competitor intercept clicks assumed navigational despite negatives.
- Demo-to-close rate: Not estimated — first campaign, no baseline data.
