# Landing Page Brief -- Bloom

> **Status: Draft** | Generated by /landing on 2026-03-19

Four dedicated landing pages, one per ad group, each with a single job: convert the visitor who clicked a specific ad into a free trial signup. Message match is everything -- the H1 should feel like a direct continuation of the headline they clicked.

---

**Campaign:** Bloom - Search (Non-Brand)
**Ad Groups:** 4 (Onboarding Software, Manager Coaching, Competitor Intercept, Retention)

---

## Design Principles

- **One CTA per page.** Primary: "Start Free Trial" (above fold). Secondary: "Book a Demo" (below fold).
- **Message match.** Keyword searched and ad clicked should be echoed in H1 and first paragraph.
- **Outcome > feature.** Lead with what changes, then show how.
- **Social proof early.** "180+ companies" or specific testimonial within first scroll.
- **No navigation distractions.** Logo + CTA only. Remove full site nav.
- **Mobile-first.** 60%+ of traffic will be mobile.

## Page Template

1. **Hero** -- H1 (keyword-matched), subhead (value prop), primary CTA, product screenshot
2. **Social proof bar** -- "Trusted by 180+ companies" + logos
3. **3 Benefits** -- Outcome-focused cards matching persona pain points
4. **How it works** -- 3 steps (Connect HRIS / Customize Workflows / Track Outcomes)
5. **Testimonial** -- Specific to the use case if available
6. **FAQ** -- 3-4 objection-handling questions
7. **Final CTA** -- Repeat primary CTA + "Book a Demo" secondary

---

## Brand Guardrails (from context/brand.md)

- Lead with outcome, never technology
- No banned words (see brand.md Forbidden Language)
- Persona-matched language (VP People, HR Leader)
- Specific proof points within 2 sentences of any claim
